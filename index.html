Who I Am

Anastasia Yang is a passionate 4th-year student at the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA) where she studies Computer Science and Engineering. Before she attended UCLA, she was a model student at Santiago Canyon College in Orange, where she earned her 5 associate degrees. She is a driven and ambitious individual who keeps her head up in everything she does, often bringing positive energy that helps to influence those around her. Now that she's at UCLA, she has been focusing her efforts on her studies and the various projects that she finds interest in. In her spare time, she enjoys a lot of different hobbies and has indulged in the creation of arts throughout her life. Most notably, she has found a passion for film making, writing, music, tarot reading, crocheting, and more. Website Creation - 2026

Arts Participation in Relation to Sociodemographics

This website showcases the relationship between arts participation and sociodemographics within the US. It was a Digital Humanities project I did with a team to explore and research more in depth the effect of arts participation in general. It was built using HTML and CSS, with the template coming from HTML5Up.
Discord Bot Development - 2026

ESA Every1s

This is a discord bot that I created for the Engineering Student Association (ESA) at UCLA club. It was created using Javascript with the Discord.js library. This bot helps to automate a game played throughout the club where it pairs up two random members, and in a week, they have to meet up together to gain points. It supports 4 different commands to pair up members (/every1s), submits the pair together (/submit), checks the leaderboard (/leaderboard), and resets the pairings (/reset).

Game Development - 2026

A Helping Paw

This is a simple game created using Unity with a team for a Game Jam, in which we won 3rd place in. You play as an old woman doing daily household tasks only to find out that you just seem to always forget if you've done a task. I was both a game developer and also the main writer for the game. The theme for the Game Jam was "on the horizon", having been judged by several famous game developers, who helped to give feedback towards how we can continue improving the game.
Front End Development - 2025 to 2026

ESA Club Finder

This website was created to help UCLA engineering students locate active clubs within the Samueli school of Engineering, made by the club Engineering Student Association (ESA) at UCLA. It was built using React.js and Node.js with MongoDB for the database. I first helped with the front end development of the login, home, and club pages, but then later helped with the back end development and helping with the deployment of the website.

Development - 2024

Blackjack in Java

This is a simple implementation of the classic Blackjack game done in Java using JOptionPane for the GUI. It was a project done on the side, allowing for continuous development for different games with the way that the cards were shuffled. It supports 1 bot dealer, and 1-4 players (having a replacement of bots for any players not present).
